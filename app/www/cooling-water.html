Know your water cooling requirement

Furnace design and today's run in, cooling water out: heat load per zone (hearth bottom, hearth wall, tuyere belt, bosh, belly, stack, throat), tuyeres and tuyere coolers, the flow each circuit needs from heat (Q = ṁ·cp·ΔT) and from the minimum water velocity, pump, make-up and emergency tank sizes, and alarm limits. Check running compares measured flows and ΔT with the design.

Starting values are typical published ranges, not your OEM's design. Replace them with the stave / tuyere supplier's data and follow plant SOP.
