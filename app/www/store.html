How to install

  1. Tap Download for your computer. The file opens in your browser or Google Drive: choose Download there.
  2. Windows: unzip the folder, then open Iron Making.exe. Mac: unzip, drag the app to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time.
  3. Office PC (no install): unzip the folder and open READ ME - how to start.txt. Nothing is installed.
  4. Open the app and log in with your login — the same login you use here.
