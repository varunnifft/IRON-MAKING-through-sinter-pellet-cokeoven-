IRON MAKING 5.8 for Mac
Designed & developed by Niteen Kumar
Needs macOS 11 Big Sur or newer.

1. Double-click the downloaded zip: you get "Iron Making.app".
2. Drag "Iron Making.app" into your Applications folder.
3. First start: double-click it. macOS says it cannot verify the developer
   (the app is not notarised with a paid Apple certificate).
   - macOS 15 Sequoia / 14 Sonoma / 13 Ventura: click Done, then open
     System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway",
     enter your password, and click Open.
   - macOS 12 and older: right-click (Control-click) the app > Open > Open.
   If it says the app "is damaged", open Terminal and run:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Iron Making.app"
   then open it again.
4. After the first time it opens normally from Launchpad or the Dock.

Log in with your BF Model account, or choose Continue without an account.
Your inputs are saved on this Mac (not shared with the phone app).
Menu: Go > Iron Making home / BF Model / Sinter / Coke / Carbon;  File > Print / Save page as PDF.
