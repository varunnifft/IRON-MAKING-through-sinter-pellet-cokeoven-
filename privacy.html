Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Iron Making app (formerly BF Model), developed by Niteen Kumar, collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes information. It covers the Android app (package com.bfburdenmodel.ironmaking), the website at varunnifft.github.io and the Windows/Mac version. App home page: varunnifft.github.io/IRON-MAKING-through-sinter-pellet-cokeoven-/

1. Information we collect

  • Account details you give us: your name, company or plant, login ID and password, and, if you enter them, your email address and mobile number.
  • Information from Google sign-in: if you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app, together with a Google account ID inside a signed ID token. These are the only Google permissions (scopes) the app requests: openid, email and profile. We never receive your Google password.
  • Your work in the app: the inputs, files and results you save (for example burden, sinter, coke and planner data), feedback and suggestions you send, and your learning-module and exam progress.
  • Basic usage: app version, device type (Android, Windows, Mac or browser), the section you open and the time of sign-in.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, call logs, Gmail, Google Drive files or any other Google account data. The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

2. How we use Google user data

Your Google name, email address and profile picture are used only to:

  • create your Iron Making account and sign you in;
  • show your name and picture inside the app;
  • let you recover your account and contact you about your account or app updates.

Iron Making's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not use it to build profiles for anyone else, and we do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models. No person reads it except as needed to support your account, for security, or where the law requires.

3. How we use your other information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To back up your saved work so it is not lost if you change phone or computer.
  • To let the app owner approve or block access, including downloads for Windows/Mac.
  • To tell you when a new version, quiz or exam is available.
  • To answer feedback and fix problems.

4. Where your information is stored

Your work is saved on your own device. Your account details and a backup copy of your saved work are stored in the app owner's Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive, reached through Google Apps Script) at varunnifft@gmail.com. Login passwords are stored only as a secure hash, never as plain text. Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not share it with third parties. The only exceptions are: Google, which hosts the storage as our service provider under its own terms; and disclosure where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and backed-up data for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account details and backed-up data from our storage within 30 days. If an account is not used for 3 years, we may delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can use the app with a login ID instead of Google sign-in.
  • To see, correct or delete your account and backed-up data, email varunnifft@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We will act within 30 days.
  • You can remove Iron Making's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data saved on your phone.

8. Notifications and permissions

On Android, the app may ask permission to send notifications about updates and quizzes, and to open files you choose for import. You can turn these off at any time in your phone settings.

9. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The website itself does not use cookies or collect personal information. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP address) under its own privacy statement.

10. Children

Iron Making is a professional tool for the iron and steel industry and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
