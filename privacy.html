Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what the Iron Making app (Android app, website and Windows/Mac version) collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, company or plant, login ID and, if you give them, your email address and mobile number.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app. We never receive your Google password.
  • Your work in the app: the inputs, files and results you save (for example burden, sinter, coke and planner data), and your learning-module progress.
  • Basic usage: app version, the section you open and the time of sign-in, used to manage access and send update notices.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To back up your work so it is not lost if you change phone.
  • To let the app owner approve or block access, including access from Windows/Mac.
  • To tell you when a new version or quiz is available.

Where it is stored

Your work is saved on your device. A backup copy and your account details are stored in the app owner's Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive, through Google Apps Script). Login passwords are stored only as a secure hash.

Sharing

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it. Data is handled by Google as the storage provider, under Google's own terms.

Notifications

On Android, the app may ask permission to send notifications about updates and quizzes. You can turn them off at any time in your phone settings.

Your choices

  • You can use the app with a login ID instead of Google sign-in.
  • To see, correct or delete your account and backed-up data, email varunnifft@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We will act within 30 days.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data saved on your phone.

Children

Iron Making is a professional tool for the iron and steel industry and is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
