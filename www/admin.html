Sign in with Google

Lets users sign in by picking a Google account on their phone. Paste your OAuth Client ID (Google Cloud → APIs & Services → Credentials → Web application) and the link of google-login.html on your GitHub Pages. Leave both empty to switch it off. "Continue with email" (6-digit code to any address) works without this.

🔔 Phone app update notice

After the new version is live on Google Play, type its number here and tap Publish. Every phone with an older version gets an Update available box at once and every hour until it updates, plus an Android notification about every hour (works from app 6.3.6 onwards, if the user allows notifications). Clear it to stop the reminders.

🔒 Lock · App for Win / Mac / PC / Laptop

You choose who may download and open the Windows, Mac and Office PC copies. Allow gives one user access; Deny stops only the computer version (they can still log in to the phone app); Reset removes your decision so the lock switch decides. Computer copies already in use stop at their next daily check.

Download access requests

Users who opened App for Win / Mac / PC / Laptop and asked for access. You also get an email for each request. Allow lets them download and open the computer version; Deny refuses the computer version only; Block denies log-in to the whole app; Reject deletes the row. Already-allowed users do not show here.

App for Win / Mac / PC / Laptop

Every version is free for all users. Upload the zip to Google Drive (or OneDrive / GitHub), set the share to Anyone with the link, and paste that link here. Users then see a Download button for it. Change a link any time: users always get the current one.
